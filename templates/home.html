01 / ABOUT PHISHGUARD

One security suite.
Multiple phishing vectors.

Phishing attacks can arrive through websites, QR codes, emails and other digital communication channels. PhishGuard brings multiple detection capabilities into one centralized security environment.

What is PhishGuard?

PhishGuard is an AI-powered cybersecurity application developed to help identify and analyze potential phishing threats. The system examines suspicious digital inputs, calculates a risk level and provides security-focused analysis to help users understand the potential threat.

Why multi-vector detection?

Modern phishing attacks are not limited to traditional website links. Attackers can use QR codes, deceptive email messages and malicious URLs to trick users. PhishGuard provides a unified platform for analyzing these different attack vectors.
